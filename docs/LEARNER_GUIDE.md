# Participant guide

How to use the Crew Scaler Global Cohort learning workspace as a participant (learner).
Button and page names below are the labels you see on screen.

Good to know first:
- The interface is in English only for now. Course content is shown as written by your
  instructors and is never machine-translated.
- Course updates appear in **Activity** only. No course notification emails are sent. The
  only emails are invitations, password resets and email confirmation.
- Completing a course here does not by itself grant a certificate, credit or accreditation.

## 1. Your account

Accounts are created by invitation from a program administrator; there is no public sign-up.
An account without any enrollment is a **Registered Online Account**: you can sign in, edit
your profile and browse the **Course Catalog**, but you cannot open private courses or cohorts.

### Accept an invitation and set your password
1. Open the invitation email and choose **Accept invitation**.
2. On **Set your password**, enter a **New password** (at least 10 characters, with letters and
   numbers) twice and choose **Save password**.
3. On **Invitations**, choose **Accept invitation** next to your cohort or course. You are added
   right away; use **Open the course** or **Go to Courses**.

If you already have an account, a new invitation appears in **Activity** instead of by email.
Open it, or go to **Profile** → **Invitations** → **View invitations**.
- An invitation only works for the email address it was sent to. If you see "This invitation
  is not for this account", sign out and sign in with the invited address.
- If the link has expired, was already used or was revoked, ask your program administrator
  to send it again.

### Sign in and sign out
- Go to the sign-in page, enter your **Email address** and **Password**, choose **Sign in**.
- **Sign Out** is at the bottom of the navigation menu (on a phone, open the menu first).
  Always sign out on shared computers.
- "Your account is not active" means the account was suspended; contact your program administrator.

### Forgot your password
1. On the sign-in page choose **Forgot your password?**, enter your email, **Send reset link**.
2. Open the email and choose **Choose a new password**. The link works once and expires.
3. Enter the new password twice and **Save password**.

To change a password you know, use **Profile** → **Password** → **Change password**.

## 2. Getting around

The left navigation (the menu button on a phone) has your name (your **Profile**),
**Activity**, **Courses**, **Cohorts & Communities**, **Calendar**, **Messages** (with an unread
count), **Grades** and **Tools**. The round **?** button at the bottom right opens **Help**.
Times are shown in the time zone set on your profile.

## 3. Activity

- **Courses Activity**: your current courses, with **Continue learning**, unread updates and progress.
- **Upcoming deadlines**: assignments due, quizzes closing and events in the next 14 days.
- **Activity Stream**: announcements, grades, messages, content changes, returned work,
  access-request decisions and invitations. Filter by type, choose **Open** or
  **View my grade** on an item, **Mark all as read**, **Show more**.
- The settings icon opens **Notification settings** on your profile.

## 4. Courses

1. **Courses** lists courses you take or teach, grouped as Ongoing, Upcoming and closed terms.
2. Use **Search your courses**, **Cohorts / terms**, and **Filters** (All courses, Favorites,
   Courses I'm taking, Courses I teach), then **Apply**.
3. Switch between **List view** and **Grid view**, and choose how many items per page.
4. The star button (**Add to favorites** / **Remove from favorites**) adds a course to Favorites.

If the list is empty, you are not enrolled yet: browse the **Course Catalog** (section 13).

## 5. Inside a course

Tabs: **Overview**, **Content**, **Announcements**, **Assignments**, **Quizzes**,
**Discussions**, **Grades**, **Calendar**, **People**.
- **Overview**: about the course, dates (your time zone and the course time zone), course plan,
  staff, **Your progress** and the next step (**Start** or **Continue learning**).
- Progress counts required lessons only. Optional lessons never block completion.
- **People** shows names and roles only; email addresses are never shown.
- When a course has ended or your enrollment is completed, you can still review it, but
  progress and new submissions are no longer recorded.

## 6. Lessons

1. **Content** shows modules and lessons with type (Reading, PDF, Video, Download, External
   resource, Embedded video), Required/Optional, estimated time and status.
2. Open a lesson. Use **Previous lesson** / **Next lesson** or **Back to course content**.
3. Complete it:
   - Readings, PDFs, files and links: **Mark as complete**.
   - Uploaded videos with a playback rule: once you have played at least 90% of the video,
     choose **Mark as played**. This records playback, not what you learned.
   - Quizzes and assignments complete through their own results.
4. PDFs: **Download PDF** or **Open PDF in a new tab** if the preview does not display.

**Video controls**: the player's own play, seek, volume and captions control (when captions
were provided), plus **Playback speed**. Your position is saved; when you return it says
"Resumed at …", and **Start from the beginning** restarts. **Show transcript** appears when the
instructor added one. Embedded YouTube or Vimeo videos are hosted by those providers.

**Locked lessons** show **Locked** and the reason, for example:
- the course has not started yet, or the lesson opens on a set date;
- "Complete “…” first" (another lesson);
- "Score at least N% on “…”": this uses your **released** quiz result, so it opens only after
  your instructor publishes the result.
If you already completed a lesson that becomes locked again, your completion still counts.

## 7. Quizzes

1. **Quizzes** lists each quiz with availability, time limit, attempts and result. Choose
   **Open quiz** (or **Resume attempt**).
2. Before starting, the page shows opening and closing times, the time limit, attempts left,
   how scoring works and the exact deadline: "If you start now, you must submit by …".
   If the quiz closes before your full time limit, it tells you how many minutes you will have.
3. **Start attempt N** → **Start now**. This uses one attempt.
4. Answers save automatically ("Saved at …"). The timer runs on the server, even if you close
   the tab or lose your connection. If you are offline, keep the tab open; answers save when
   you reconnect.
5. To continue later, open the quiz again and choose **Resume attempt N**.
6. **Submit attempt** → **Submit now**. You cannot change answers after submitting.
7. When the time limit or closing time passes, the server submits the answers saved so far.

**Results and review**: short answers are graded by staff, so a result can show "Pending manual
grading". Scores usually appear when staff publish them. Whether you can see correct answers
depends on the quiz: after submitting, after the quiz closes, when staff release them, or never.
Use **Review attempt N** when it is available. Practice quizzes do not count toward your grade.
If you need extra time as an accommodation, ask your instructor before the quiz opens.

## 8. Assignments

1. Open the assignment from **Assignments**. Read the details, due and closing times, late work
   policy, rubric and how many submissions you may make.
2. Add your work: **Text submission**, a link starting with https://, and/or **Add files**
   (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, images, text or code files; code is stored as text and never run).
3. **Save draft** keeps your work private; staff cannot see drafts.
4. **Submit** (or **Submit version N**). You get a receipt: "Receipt … · version … · time".
   Work after the due date is marked late, or refused if late work is not accepted.
5. You can submit again up to the limit while the work is not graded. After grading, you can
   resubmit only if your instructor chooses **Return for revision**; you are notified in Activity.
   Every version is kept in **Submission history**.

If the connection fails while submitting, submit again: if it already arrived you get the same
receipt, not a new version.

## 9. Grades

- **Grades** lists your current and past courses with a **Running total from released
  grades** and **View all work (N)**.
- You see a grade only after course staff publish it. Graded but unpublished work shows as
  "Graded, not yet published".
- The total is points earned divided by points possible for counted, released items only.
  **Missing** counts as 0, **Exempt** is left out, and unpublished items are left out of both
  numbers until they are released. Feedback and rubric scores appear with each released item.

## 10. Messages

1. **Messages** groups conversations by course and cohort. You can message people who share
   that course or cohort with you.
2. **New Message** → choose the course or cohort → pick people under **To** → **Subject**,
   **Message**, optional attachments → **Send**. Sending to several people asks you to confirm.
3. Reply in a conversation with **Send reply**. The page checks for new messages automatically.
Only people in a conversation can read it and its attachments. No email copies are sent.

## 11. Announcements and discussions

- **Announcements** (course tab, and cohort announcements on the cohort page) are posted by
  staff. Pinned ones stay on top.
- **Discussions**: staff open course topics. Use **Add a post** → **Post**, **Reply** →
  **Post reply**, and **Edit** your own posts (earlier versions are kept). Markdown formatting
  works. Locked topics are read-only; a moderator may hide a post with a reason.

## 12. Calendar, cohorts and communities

**Calendar** shows assignment due dates, quiz windows, live sessions, office hours and events.
- Switch **Month**, **Week** or **List**; use **Today** and the arrows; filter by course or
  cohort and item type, then **Apply**.
- Open an item for details and links (**Join meeting** for an external meeting link).
- **Download .ics** adds a one-time copy to your calendar app; download again if it changes.
  There is no live calendar subscription.

**Cohorts & Communities**: your cohorts with their course offerings, staff, participants
(names only), announcements, discussions and shortcuts to the cohort calendar and tools.
**Communities** are optional groups: **Join community** / **Leave community**. Some are
invitation only. Joining a community never gives access to a course.

## 13. Course Catalog and requesting access

1. Open **Course Catalog** (from **Courses**, or your profile if you have no enrollments).
2. Search and filter by **Cohort**, **Term** and **Availability**, then **Apply**.
3. For an offering marked **Open for requests**: **Request access**, optionally add a message,
   **Send request**. You are not enrolled until program staff approve; the decision appears in
   Activity. You can **Withdraw request** while it is pending.
Offerings marked **Not open for requests** are by invitation only.

## 14. Tools

**Tools** lists technical setup guides, cohort policies, support details and links approved by
program staff, filtered by scope and category. External links open in a new tab.

## 15. Profile

- **Account**: your email (visible only to you and program administrators; ask an
  administrator to change it), account type, enrollments and roles, **Invitations**.
- **Personal details**: **Display name** (accented and Vietnamese names are supported), **Bio**,
  **Preferred language** (saved for later; the interface is English only for now), **Time
  zone** (search, or **Use this device's time zone**) → **Save details**.
- **Profile photo**: PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 5 MB → **Upload a new photo**; **Remove photo**.
  If uploads are held for review on your platform, a new photo cannot be used yet.
- **Notifications**: choose which updates appear in Activity → **Save notification settings**.
  Email notifications are not available.
- **Password**: current password, new password twice → **Change password**.

## 16. Help and support

- **Help** (the **?** button) covers signing in, courses, uploads, quizzes, grades and support,
  and lists accepted file types and size limits when you are signed in.
- For course content, deadlines or grades, message your instructor from **Messages**.
- The program support contact appears on Help once an administrator has set it. Until then
  Help says "Support contact not configured yet".
- Privacy, Terms and Accessibility pages are linked at the bottom of the navigation. They are
  drafts awaiting owner review.

Sample accounts ending in `@sample.crewscaler.test` exist only in local and staging test data.
